Commodity Intelligence

An invented copper market. Official figures arrive two months late; shipping, stocks and prices arrive at once. A nowcast of the supply-demand balance that says which signal moved it, change points on cargo flows, and a scenario engine that separates structure from a price call. …
The market, its mines, ports, cargoes, prices and official statistics are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real market or real data, and nothing here is trading advice. No outside model or service is called.
